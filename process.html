The Journey

Five Phases.
One Flawless Space.

  1. 01
    Phase One

    Discovery & Consultation

    Every project begins with listening. We visit your space (or conduct a detailed virtual walkthrough) and spend time understanding your lifestyle, aesthetic references, functional needs, and most importantly — your timeline requirements.

    Deliverables
    • Site measurement & assessment report
    • Initial mood board & style direction
    • Preliminary scope & budget range
    • Agreed project timeline document
    Typical Duration 3–5 Days
    Free for new clients
  2. 02
    Phase Two

    Concept Design & 3D Visualisation

    Our designers translate your vision into detailed 2D floor plans and photorealistic 3D renders. You see exactly how your finished space will look — lighting, materials, furniture placement — before a single nail is driven.

    Deliverables
    • 2D floor plan with dimensions
    • Photorealistic 3D renders (all rooms)
    • Material & finish selection board
    • Furniture & joinery specifications
    Typical Duration 7–10 Days
  3. 03
    Phase Three

    Approval & Procurement

    Once designs are approved, we issue a detailed, itemised quote. We handle all procurement — sourcing materials, ordering custom furniture, coordinating vendor lead times — so the execution phase can begin without delays.

    Deliverables
    • Finalised, itemised cost breakdown
    • Signed project agreement & timeline
    • Material samples for in-person approval
    • Custom furniture production initiated
    Typical Duration 5–7 Days
  4. 04
    Phase Four

    Execution & Site Management

    This is where ARG truly differentiates. Our dedicated site supervisor manages all civil work, electrical, plumbing, false ceiling, flooring, painting, and installation on a daily basis. You receive weekly photo and video progress reports directly to WhatsApp.

    What We Manage
    • Civil & structural work coordination
    • Electrical, plumbing & HVAC oversight
    • False ceiling, flooring & wall treatments
    • Custom furniture & fixture installation
    Typical Duration 4–10 Weeks (scope-dependent)
    Weekly progress updates on WhatsApp
  5. 05
    Phase Five

    Handover & Post-Support

    We don't just hand over keys — we walk you through your space room by room, explaining care instructions for every material and surface. A comprehensive snagging check is completed, and any final touches are resolved before we consider the project closed.

    Deliverables
    • Room-by-room walkthrough session
    • Material care & maintenance guide
    • Warranty documentation for all products
    • 30-day free snagging & aftercare
    Typical Duration 1 Day + 30-Day Support Period
    30-day aftercare included

Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you handle everything, or do we need to hire contractors separately? +

ARG is a fully integrated studio. We manage everything in-house — design, civil work, electrical, plumbing, custom furniture production, and installation. You have one point of contact and one team accountable for the entire project from start to finish.

What if I want changes after the 3D design is approved? +

Two rounds of design revisions are included at no cost in the design phase. Once execution begins, structural or layout changes may involve additional costs, which we will always communicate and get approved before proceeding. We never surprise you with a bill.

How is the payment structured? +

We work on a milestone-based payment structure: typically 30% at project sign-off, 30% at material procurement, 30% during execution milestones, and 10% at final handover. This ensures both parties are aligned and there are no surprises at any stage.

Do you take on projects outside Pune? +

Our primary focus is the greater Pune region including Lohegaon, Magarpatta, Kharadi, Baner, Hinjewadi, and surrounding areas. For projects in other cities, please contact us to discuss feasibility — we evaluate on a case-by-case basis.

What is the minimum budget you work with? +

We are a premium studio and our projects typically start from ₹8–10 lakh for a 2BHK and scale upward based on scope and material choices. During the free consultation, we will give you a realistic range for your specific requirements without obligation.

Do you provide a warranty on the completed work? +

Yes. All ARG projects include a 30-day post-handover snagging period at no additional cost. Beyond that, our materials and custom furniture carry manufacturer warranties which we document and hand over to you as part of our handover package.
